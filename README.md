# Tidybo

**A tiny robot that tidies your Mac desktop.** Select some files, click the robot above the
Dock, and watch a little robot vacuum slide out of the camera notch, drive over each file and
suck it into a folder.

> ⚠️ **Tidybo really moves your files.** Selected desktop items are moved into a new
> **Tidybo Folder N** on your desktop. Nothing is ever deleted or overwritten. Changed your
> mind? Just drag the files back out of the folder.

## Install

**[⬇️ Download the latest release](https://github.com/astapable/Tidybo/releases/latest)**
(macOS 14 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel)

1. Download **Tidybo-…zip** and double-click it to unzip.
2. Drag **Tidybo.app** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the developer, because Tidybo isn't notarized by
   Apple. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
   **Open Anyway** next to Tidybo.

## Permissions

Tidybo needs **Accessibility** access to see where your desktop icons are:

**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility** → turn on **Tidybo**, then open
Tidybo again.

## Use

1. Select files on your desktop.
2. Hover the thin capsule above the Dock and click the robot.

Start and quit it like any Mac app: from the Dock, or with ⌘Q.
